A Little About Me

Design Thinking. Technical Curiosity. Purposeful Results.

I am Anna Hovsepyan, a Shopify Developer passionate about building elegant, functional and user-friendly eCommerce experiences.

My journey into web development began with an interest in the relationship between design, technology and business. I enjoy turning creative ideas into structured digital experiences.

I work with Shopify, Liquid, HTML, CSS and JavaScript to customize storefronts, build tailored content sections and refine website layouts.

My current portfolio includes an independent luxury beauty eCommerce concept, where I have explored brand presentation, custom styling, page structure and Shopify customization.

I value clear communication, continuous learning, attention to detail and a thoughtful approach to every development task.

Creating with intention. Developing with purpose.
