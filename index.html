About Me

A Passion for Building Meaningful Digital Experiences.

Hello, I'm Anna Hovsepyan, a Shopify Developer focused on creating visually refined and user-friendly eCommerce websites.

My journey into eCommerce development began with a genuine interest in the intersection of design, technology and business. I enjoy understanding how a brand communicates visually and translating that identity into an engaging online shopping experience.

I work with Shopify, Liquid, HTML, CSS and JavaScript to customize themes, develop tailored sections and improve storefront presentation and functionality.

I believe that successful eCommerce development requires more than attractive visuals. It requires structure, attention to detail, usability, responsiveness and a clear understanding of the customer's journey.

My approach is thoughtful, detail-oriented and focused on delivering work that supports the client's vision and business objectives.

"Great digital experiences are created when beautiful design meets purposeful technology."
